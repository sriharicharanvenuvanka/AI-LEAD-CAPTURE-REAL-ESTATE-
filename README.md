# Real Estate Lead Capture & Qualification Automation

## Overview
An ongoing project focused on streamlining real estate lead management through automated lead capture, qualification, scoring, and follow-up preparation. The workflow processes incoming property inquiries, validates lead information, identifies existing customers, maintains CRM records, and classifies leads as Hot, Warm, or Cold based on predefined scoring criteria. High-priority leads are routed for AI-assisted email drafting to support timely and personalized follow-up.

## Workflow
- Capture and process incoming property inquiries.
- Normalize and validate lead information.
- Identify new and existing leads and maintain customer records.
- Calculate lead scores and assign priority categories.
- Generate personalized email drafts for hot leads.
- Update CRM records with relevant lead and follow-up information.
